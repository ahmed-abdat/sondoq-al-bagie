# Background

**رابطة شباب قرية البقيع** is a village youth association with about 71 members and a WhatsApp group of the same name. Its fund pays for summer cultural activities (tutoring, honouring national exam winners) and supports the sports team.

**How it works today (2026):**
- Members pay a subscription every month: group A 1000 MRO, group B 500 MRO.
- They transfer money with local wallets (Bankily, Masrvi, Sedad), post the screenshot in the WhatsApp group, and a committee member ticks their name on a paper sheet.
- The paper sheet "صندوق رابطة شباب البقيع 2026" numbers members 1..70+, with 12 month columns and a tick per paid month. Each page has a handwritten total.
  - Page 1 (members 1–21, group A): total 179000 = exactly 179 ticks × 1000.
  - Pages 28–53 total 60000 and 54–70 total 36000, which fits 500/month (inferred).
- The fund is run by an elected committee of 3–6 people. One of them, the treasurer, holds the money in their personal wallet. AHMED is on this term's committee.

**What AHMED wants:** digitization, transparency and easy access. The committee manages the fund, confirms payments, sends reminders and records expenses. Members see the balance and who is up to date or behind, upload proof easily, pay for a year or part of it, and can pay for a relative.

The paper-sheet photos and receipt screenshots are not in this repo because they contain members' personal data. Ask AHMED for the 2026 sheet data when doing the M1 import.
