# UI review: committee-only app (m2-ui @ 97fd5d7), 2026-09-30

Read-only review. Fixtures build (`SONDOQ_FIXTURES=1`), Playwright at 360, 390, 1280, plus a 180 px viewport at
2× to stand in for 200% zoom. Method: impeccable critique/audit, done in a single context (the detector
was run separately and gave advisory findings only).
Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-ahmede-Documents-dev-web-sondoq-al-bagie--claude-worktrees-app/36d5b38c-bf04-4082-b764-81d7d230f8bb/scratchpad/shots/`
(`_committee*-360|390.png` = every route, `flow360-*` = record/undo/sheets/focus/200%, `st-*` = states,
`desk_*` = 1280, `zoom-*` = close-ups, `proto*` = approved prototype).

## Verdict

The **new** screens (home, سجّل دفعة, المزيد, سجل العمليات) are good. They follow the approved prototype
closely: flat tonal surfaces, plain Arabic, 48 px targets, correct RTL chevrons, a visible focus ring
and no sideways scroll at 360. Member search works for «ب 2», «ب2», «B-2», «2» and «ب ٢».

**The app as a whole is not ready to show the committee.**
- Three of the five tabs (الأعضاء, التبرعات, التقارير) and most المزيد entries still open the **old**
  screens. Those screens break the owner rules: a confirmation queue, «ما يراه الأعضاء», the old roles,
  «مقدَّمًا» and «X من Y».
- The campaign and لوحة rows on home lead to a **404 page in English**.
- A CSS clash makes headings **overlap their text** on the old screens.

## Matches the approved prototype? (proto/admin a2df781, `/prototype/admin/set`)

- **Home (B):** yes, almost pixel for pixel (`proto_prototype_admin_set-360.png` vs `_committee-360.png`).
  The copy is the same, so the «وبقي» ambiguity (row P1 above) came from the prototype.
- **Record (C2):** yes. Member first, quick month chips, relatives, several people, «كيف دفع؟» cards, sticky
  total. The app adds «متبرع من خارج الصندوق» to the add row (useful, but adds choices). Relatives show
  status («دفع حتى مايو») instead of the prototype's «أ 15 · 4 000 أوقية»; the app's version is kinder and
  in line with no shaming. The back link says «الرئيسية» instead of the member's name when opened from a
  member (fine until the members tab is new).
- **المزيد:** partly. The list of entries differs (see P2 row above). «تنتظر التأكيد» is not in the prototype.
- **Members (A), Campaigns (B), Reports (B3):** **no.** Production still serves the old screens. The
  prototype's reports tab (الإحصاءات, «للمجموعة» ×6, «للجنة» ×3) is not reachable, although
  `Reports3` is already in `src/components/admin/reports3.tsx`.
- **Owner rules:** the new screens follow them (✓/empty month tiles, no «X من 12», Western digits,
  48 px, flat tonal surfaces). The old screens do not («42 من 88», «مقدَّمًا», confirmation queue, member
  visibility switches).
